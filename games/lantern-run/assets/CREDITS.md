# Asset credits

All game art is drawn in code at runtime by `src/art-parts.ts` and `src/art-world.ts` with Canvas shapes and gradients. Character and prop parts are pre-rendered at twice their logical size; the wide parallax backgrounds use native resolution. All sound and music is synthesized at runtime by `src/audio.ts`.

`cover.webp` was generated by the operator with OpenArt (GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst) for this project on 1 November 2026, from the project's own concept art with the buildings removed, and is used with the operator's permission. The PNG was resized to 800×1200 and encoded as WebP for the Library. All in-game art remains drawn in code; no concept image is loaded by the game.
